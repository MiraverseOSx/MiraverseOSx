### Summary: paste this text

At approximately 08:41 on [INCIDENT DATE], neighbor Dena Banks called emergency services after finding Lily Preyana Moss on the kitchen floor with a bleeding head injury. An earlier call had gone unanswered, and the front door was ajar. Lily was transferred for assessment.

Stella Vale was not found at the address. Julian Denise Vale, Lily's separated husband, is also unaccounted for. Santiago Whitmore, Lily's current partner, has been identified for contact. The circumstances of the injury and the missing persons' departure remain unconfirmed.

The immediate priorities are to locate Julian and Stella, establish their welfare, preserve the scene, and verify the household's expected movements and visitors. No offender has been identified.
