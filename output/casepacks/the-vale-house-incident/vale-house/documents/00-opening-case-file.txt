SUMMARY OF THE MATTER
At approximately 08:41 local time, neighbor Dena Banks contacted emergency services after finding Lily Preyana Moss on the kitchen floor of her residence with a bleeding head injury. Banks reports that the front door was ajar and that an earlier call to Moss had gone unanswered. Responders arranged Lily's transfer for assessment.

Stella Vale, a child associated with the household, was not found at the address. Julian Denise Vale, Lily's separated husband, is also unaccounted for. His presence at the residence earlier that morning is a lead to be verified. Lily's current partner, Santiago Whitmore, has been identified for contact.

No offender has been identified at the opening of this file. The absence of Julian and Stella does not establish that they left voluntarily or that Julian caused Lily's injuries. Their immediate whereabouts and welfare remain the priority.
