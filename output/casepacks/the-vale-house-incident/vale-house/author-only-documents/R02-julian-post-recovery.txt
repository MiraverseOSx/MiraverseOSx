Lily asked me to come over. We are separated, but we still discuss Stella and practical things. She wanted another pair of eyes on some accommodation dates. I arrived a little before the time she had suggested.

The Northline woman came with a folder. Lily knew who she was expecting. She wanted the pages Lily had copied. Lily told her there was another set for her editor. The conversation changed after that.

I remember Lily trying to keep hold of her folder. The woman struck and pushed her. Lily fell in the kitchen. I said we needed help. The woman threatened that things would get worse if I did not do what she said. Stella was there. I believed refusing would put them both in further danger.

I drove the gray car. That was not a journey I had planned with Stella. The woman sat beside me and directed where we went. She made me send Santiago the message about Northgate. We did not go to Northgate. I did not write that because I wanted to disappear with my daughter.

At the annex, I asked to call Lily and to leave. We were kept waiting and were not allowed to go. I kept telling Stella that somebody would find us. I do not know everything the staff had been told. I can describe the people I spoke to, but I do not know their names.

I had not met Mara Vey before that morning. Lily's appointment note and the name used at the house are how I knew who she was. I can assist with a properly recorded identification, but I should not be shown a single photograph and told that it must be her.
