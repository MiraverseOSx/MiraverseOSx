# The Vale House Incident

An investigation case pack for document generators.

Lily Moss is found injured in her kitchen. Her daughter Stella and her separated husband Julian are missing. A message from Julian's phone suggests he has taken Stella away, but the physical and documentary evidence tells a different story.

## Established details retained

- Julian Denise Vale and Lily Preyana Moss are named on the supplied marriage certificate. The certificate is accepted as-is. Its ages describe that record; this pack does not assign present-day ages from them.
- Lily and Julian are separated. Santiago Whitmore is Lily's current romantic partner.
- Stella Vale is the missing child. This pack proposes that Julian and Lily are her parents; the certificate alone does not establish parentage.
- Dena Banks is the neighbor who found Lily after an unanswered call, with the front door ajar. Lily was on the kitchen floor with a bleeding head injury; Stella was not there. Dena called for help.
- Julian is a security administrator and Lily is a travel journalist, as recorded on the certificate.

## New story material

The incident times, Northline Relocation Services, Mara Vey, Cedar Annex R-4, Lily's reporting assignment, the evidence, and the resolution are newly authored proposals. They are not facts extracted from the certificate or existing Miraverse characters. The plot is grounded in ordinary coercion, records, and witness accounts; it does not require supernatural knowledge or an unseen mastermind.

The screenshot's Pickman Street address, old 1920s dates, Silas Gedney portrait caption, and unrelated sample file contents are not carried into this case.

## Files and reading order

1. Read `01-STORY-AND-CAST.md` for the premise and character direction.
2. Give the investigator `documents/00-opening-case-file.txt` and `documents/E01-dena-banks-statement.txt`.
3. Use `GENERATOR-GUIDE.csv` to choose templates and paste in the corresponding text. The original certificate is `evidence/E02-marriage-certificate.pdf`.
4. Release the remaining documents as the investigator follows leads. See `02-RELEASE-AND-INVESTIGATION.md`.
5. Keep `AUTHOR-ONLY-SOLUTION.md`, `AUTHOR-ONLY-TIMELINE.csv`, and `author-only-documents/` away from the investigator initially. They reveal the solution. The R01 and R02 texts are available for generating the final recovery documents after the location has been found.

`documents/` contains investigator-facing copy only. Its internal exhibit references correspond to the guide. The generator labels, such as "People involved" and "Summary," are instructions for filling that template, rather than part of a signed witness statement.

The opening packet is deliberately incomplete. It should not tell the investigator that a particular suspect committed the offense or where the missing people are being held.

## Intended outcome

The investigator locates Julian and Stella alive, establishes that Julian's departure was coerced, and supports a case against Mara Vey for Lily's assault and the detention of Julian and Stella. Final statements and a documented recovery strengthen the reconstruction; the opening evidence does not establish every detail by itself.

This is an authored case pack, not a new playable mission installed into Miraverse. No existing character files or player saves are changed.
