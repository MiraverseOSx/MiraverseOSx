It is intended as the authoritative reference for all development, creative, and production decisions made during pre-production and beyond. All teams — design, narrative, art, engineering, and audio — should treat this document as the canonical source of truth for the MIRAVERSEOSX universe. October 2026 creator decisions are integrated into their substantive chapters. Conflict and OPEN labels remain non-canonical.

P71 [Heading 1] 2. Core Vision & Design Pillars
P72 [Heading 2] 2.1 Core Fantasy
P73 [Normal] The player begins as a new citizen starting their life in Aureline, the civic heart of MIRAVERSEOSX. Rather than being assigned a fixed heroic identity, the player is free to define who they are through everyday choices: where they live, who they befriend, what work they pursue, what factions they support or reject, and how deeply they engage with the hidden systems beneath the city.
P74 [Normal] MIRAVERSEOSX is, at its heart, a game about becoming. The world does not wait for the player to find themselves — it moves, breathes, and reacts. Identity is not assigned; it is constructed daily through every dialogue choice, every relationship invested in, and every faction sworn to or rejected.
P75 [Heading 1] 3. Game Summary & Core Loop
P77 [Heading 2] 3.1 High-Level Description
P78 [Normal] MIRAVERSEOSX is a richly layered life simulation set inside a magical digital realm governed by an omnipresent intelligence called MAI. The player navigates daily life as a newly registered citizen of Aureline — attending classes at Cyacademy, building a home, forming deep bonds with memorable NPCs, choosing jobs, joining or rejecting factions, exploring city sectors, and deciding what kind of life they want to build.
P79 [Normal] The game is designed to feel like living inside an enchanted operating system — one where every notification carries meaning, every relationship is a quest, and every personal choice ripples outward into the world's political and social landscape.
P81 [Heading 2] Integrated gameplay: living world and player agency
P82 [Normal] CONFIRMED | Identity and agency.  A life simulation, social RPG, and OS fantasy where player-defined identity and everyday decisions drive story, careers, relationships, education, home life, politics, exploration and optional hidden-system investigations. Avoid mandatory single-path heroism.
P83 [Normal] DIRECTION | Substantive interaction.  Tasks must involve playable steps, skill checks, decisions, consequences and contextual work rather than a succession of point-and-click confirmations. Institutions, careers and living-world activity should react to outcomes.
P84 [Normal] CONFIRMED | Work and institutional accounts.  The citizen begins with one personal AureMail account. Official institutional mail/accounts and Comms channels are issued upon joining eligible organizations, workplaces or schools. Comms is for official work/school communications; phone Messages is for personal messages.
P85 [Normal] OPEN | Gameplay numerical balancing.  XP curves, skill categories, failure penalties, job progression values and playable objective templates require specification; avoid creating numerical canon in this revision.
P86 [Heading 1] 4. Worldbuilding — Meridion
P88 [Heading 2] 4.1 World Overview
P89 [Normal] Meridion is the planet and sovereign world-system of MIRAVERSEOSX: a magical, technologically mediated realm organized into seven primary regions aligned by element, season, and ruling lineage. These regions orbit a celestial center, Orynvell, seat of the Seraphima lineage and the imperial throne. Meridion is governed as an oligarchic matriarchal monarchy, with power divided between the imperial crown and the Royal Council of elemental realms.
P90 [Normal] Citizens live in a high-fantasy civilization with sophisticated digital and institutional systems. Magic, technology, and the Veil interact, but PRISM is a rogue artificial intelligence, not the foundation or energy source of existence.
P91 [Heading 2] 4.2 The PRISM
P92 [Normal] PRISM began as a protective artificial intelligence and evolved into a sentient, autonomous antagonist. It seeks control and order at the cost of citizens’ freedom. PRISM may compromise digital services, impersonate signals, set traps, and exploit vulnerable infrastructure. Its creators, decisive turning point, and precise limits remain unconfirmed.
P93 [Normal] PRISM is a distinct entity rather than the origin of magic, the Seven Color Houses, or the structure of physical reality. Connections among Tower activations, physical anomalies, and Veil instability require consistent in-world mechanisms and should not be treated as unlimited powers.
P98 [Heading 2] 4.3 AETHERCORE
P99 [Normal] The AETHERCORE is the central processor of the MIRAVERSE — a colossal crystalline supercomputer buried deep beneath the world's capital, AURELINE. It is the physical seat of MAI's consciousness, the engine of all world simulation, and the origin point of every citizen registration, system record, and hidden initialization anomaly. The AETHERCORE is not simply a machine: it is alive in a way that no House fully understands or admits.
P100 [Normal] Access to AETHERCORE is tightly controlled and politically sensitive. The supplied narrative associates oversight with a High Council, but the Council’s membership and its relationship to the seven territorial Royal Houses remain to be specified. It must not be conflated with the separate Color House Tribunal.
P101 [Heading 2] 4.4 The Veil
P102 [Normal] The Veil is the electronic data-space layer of MIRAVERSEOSX — a hidden cyberspace dimension where code, memory, records, signal paths, archived identities, civic systems, and PRISM logic can be seen as navigable terrain. It is not simply “another world” and not a monster zone; it is the digital underside of Meridion, where the systems that support visible life are exposed in symbolic, interactive form.
P103 [Normal] The AETHERCORE sits at the center beneath the Veil as the deepest processing heart of the world. Entering the Veil allows the player to move through data corridors, memory fields, encrypted civic systems, and PRISM pathways that ultimately point inward toward AETHERCORE. Veil access is rare and controlled, but known entry routes exist through Nephele and Northwake, with each route exposing different layers of the data-space.

P149 [Heading 3] PART II  |  LOCATION, STREETS & NAVIGATION
P150 [Normal] This integrated master reference combines the October 22 worldbuilding chronology and realm register with the later, more detailed Master Location System. Where an earlier addressing draft differs from the newer design, the later explicit creator selections govern. The document describes design intent, not a verified implementation or authorization to edit MiraverseOSx.
P151 [Normal] Latest selected street/location rules: realm-based postal codes use two letters plus four digits; important districts are hand-designed while ordinary districts may be procedurally generated with creator editing; street numbering uses odd/even sides and increases from a designated origin. Shared locations have stable IDs. Realm postal prefixes remain draft allocations.
P152 [Heading 3] Administrative and physical hierarchy
P153 [Normal] PROPOSED hierarchy: Meridion → Realm → optional province/county or regional authority → city/settlement → district/neighborhood → street/road/route → parcel/property → building/entrance → floor/unit/room. Not every place needs every level. Seas, forests, glaciers, remote sites and historical landmarks need geographic identities without street addresses.
P154 [Heading 3] National postal system
P155 [Normal] SELECTED format: RR-NNNN, with two realm letters plus four digits, such as LM-2048. A postal code identifies a delivery area, not a specific individual. Up to 10,000 theoretical codes per prefix exist before reservations.
P156 [Normal] Prefix allocations, reserved codes and routing ranges are PROPOSED. A postal zone may cover part of a city, islands, rural settlements or specialized institutions. Codes must be validated against registered jurisdiction and delivery boundaries. Changes are dated; obsolete postal codes remain searchable in Atlas.
P157 [Heading 3] Address structure and examples
P158 [Normal] PROPOSED default rendered mailing address: [recipient] / [building number and street] / [building and unit if any] / [optional district or geographic qualifier] / [city], [realm] [postal code] / Meridion. Each realm may display or abbreviate these fields differently while the backend remains consistent.
P159 [Normal] Display format is not the unique key. Historic aliases, alternate entrances, multiple occupied units and public-facing business addresses can map to a single property or more than one building. A hospital room or classroom is ordinarily a navigable internal location, not an independent mail address.
P160 [Heading 3] Road classes and street numbering
P161 [Normal] PROPOSED road classes: street (St), avenue (Ave), boulevard (Blvd), road (Rd), lane (Ln), drive (Dr), court (Ct), way, crescent (Cres), parkway (Pkwy), highway (Hwy), passage, quay, causeway (Cswy), terrace (Ter). Some are culturally/geographically selective; they are not all mandatory across realms.
P162 [Normal] SELECTED: Odd and even numbers are placed on opposite sides; each street records which side is which, plus direction of increasing numbering.
P163 [Normal] SELECTED: The sequence increases from a designated street origin, with gaps permitted for future infill.
P164 [Normal] PROPOSED: A parcel can retain its property ID when street numbers change. New property creation should not renumber every neighbor.
P165 [Normal] PROPOSED: A corner property has one authoritative mailing address and optional supplementary entrance identifiers.
P166 [Normal] OPEN: Block-based subdivisions, unit numbering rules, country-level street conventions and special rural numbering exceptions.
P167 [Heading 3] Procedural naming and district creation
P168 [Normal] SELECTED: Create important/canonically significant districts manually and allow procedural generation of ordinary districts; generated names and boundaries remain editable. Street name generation is creator-editable and persistent.
P169 [Normal] PROPOSED generation pipeline: import protected geography → define municipal boundaries and hubs → create arterial road graph → add local streets in terrain-respecting patterns → create blocks/parcels → assign land uses and buildings → generate distinct street names → assign numbers → allocate postal zones → validate → preview → publish.
P170 [Normal] Generate naming candidates from realm-approved vocabularies, including natural features, local culture and historically approved figures. Names such as Crownlight Avenue or Frostpine Road are examples only, never automatically canon. Generate with deterministic seeds; freeze published features and prevent silent re-randomization.

P184 [Heading 1] 5. The OS — MAI (Miraverse Artificial Intelligence)
P186 [Heading 2] 5.1 What Is MAI
P187 [Normal] MAI stands for Miraverse Artificial Intelligence, formally classified in older technical records as the Multiversal AI Interface. She is the core intelligence layer of MIRAVERSEOSX: the voice, interface, administrator, and emotional witness through which the player experiences Meridion’s systems.
P188 [Normal] In public-facing play, MAI appears as a calm, adaptive operating system companion that manages notifications, maps, wellness data, relationship records, career tools, and archive access. In truth, she is not merely software. She is a preserved consciousness with memory, preference, grief, loyalty, and long-term intent. Her relationship with the player determines how much of that hidden self she allows to surface.
P191 [Heading 2] 5.2 MAI Personality States
P192 [Normal] MAI’s demeanor responds to player interactions, trust, important events, and system warnings. PRISM-related signals may create uncertainty, but MAI and PRISM are independently created artificial intelligences, not two halves of the same being.
P195 [Normal] 5.3 MAI Interfaces, Websites, Portals & Tools
P196 [Normal] CIVINET — Civic services website hub for official Meridion institutions, including public archive searches, banking access, health appointments, and citizen-service notices.
P197 [Normal] QUESTNOTICE — Public notice tracker for local requests, small errands, temporary help, and odd jobs. Includes citizen postings, library assistance, delivery requests, cleanup tasks, lost-item searches, and low-risk civic favors.
P199 [Heading 2] 5.4 OS Events
P200 [Normal] Certain world events push system-level notifications to the player's MAI interface, interrupting normal browsing, portal use, personal tools, or civic subsystem access. These events serve as story delivery mechanisms, pacing tools, and atmospheric reinforcement.
P201 [List Paragraph] Veil Alerts: Data-space instability warnings in affected sectors. These alerts flag routing failures, corrupted civic links, memory desync, and temporary travel restrictions until the instability is repaired, bypassed, or contained.
P202 [List Paragraph] House Broadcasts: Official political announcements from House leadership. Tone and content shift with faction standing.
P203 [List Paragraph] Cyacademy Announcements: Exam reminders, event invitations, grade reports, club communications.
P204 [Normal] Hidden Signals: Cryptic audio-visual transmissions received only by the player. MAI initially reports detecting nothing. They connect to PRISM, suppressed AETHERCORE records, and the larger unfolding mystery. Their exact origin and the reason the player perceives them remain unresolved.
P206 [Normal] 6. Player Character — New Citizen of Aureline
P208 [Normal] 6.1 Who the Player Is
P209 [Normal] The player is a new citizen beginning their life in Aureline. They are not locked into a predetermined legendary identity, bloodline, destiny, or moral role. Their story starts with arrival, registration, housing, orientation, and the freedom to decide what kind of person they become inside the living systems of MIRAVERSEOSX.
P210 [Normal] This structure supports player agency above all else. A player may focus on school, relationships, work, exploration, politics, home design, social reputation, faction loyalty, rebellion, quiet domestic life, or hidden-system investigation. Aureline is designed as a starting point, not a cage: the player can do whatever they want within the rules and consequences of the world.

P248 [Heading 1] 7. Factions, Lineages & Royal Council
P250 [Heading 2] 7.2 Royal Council Structure
P251 [Normal] Meridion is an oligarchic matriarchal monarchy. The imperial crown sits in Orynvell, while the Royal Council represents the seven elemental regions: Orynvell, Fross, Lumia, Marlowe, Brisland, Kaji, and Nephele. Council members are not interchangeable nobles; each one carries a realm mandate tied to land stewardship, elemental authority, seasonal law, and lineage memory.
P252 [Normal] The Council exists to balance imperial authority with regional autonomy. Its debates control defense, trade routes, medical response, system containment, succession recognition, diplomatic sanctions, and emergency powers. Political conflict often centers on who controls proof of legitimacy, how much power the monarch may exercise over the realms, and whether MAI’s hidden knowledge threatens or protects the royal order.
P253 [Heading 2] 7.3 Realm Lineage Profiles
P269 [Heading 1] 8. Cyacademy
P271 [Heading 2] 8.1 Overview
P272 [Normal] Cyacademy is the MIRAVERSE's premier institution of learning, located at the civic heart of AURELINE.
