-I. The World of Meridion
-Meridion is a sovereign world of seven realms whose inhabitants live among ancient allegiances, advanced civic institutions, ritual traditions, and the unseen architecture of resonance. Its cities are not simply settlements built upon ordinary earth. Beneath their streets and beneath the narratives that societies tell about themselves runs the PRISM, a deeper ordering of memory, matter, emotion, and energy. To most citizens, life is measured in ordinary acts: study, work, friendship, home, obligation, celebration, and mourning. The extraordinary is woven through the ordinary rather than replacing it.
-Aureline, the civic and imperial heart of Orynvell, occupies an exceptional place in the country’s political and metaphysical geography. It is the site of royal legitimacy and an immense hidden structure called the AETHERCORE. Northwake and Nephele, though distant in character, are also important in accounts of rare passage into the Veil. The realms remain distinct in their climates, histories, and lineages while participating in the order established at Meridion’s founding.
-Realm and House
-Two sevenfold arrangements exist and must never be mistaken for one another. The Seven Realms are territorial and political divisions associated with royal lineages. The Seven Color Houses are social and resonant affiliations organized around different domains of human and metaphysical concern. A citizen may live in one realm, descend from a family rooted in another, be born to one Color House, and presently belong to another. None of these affiliations automatically proves membership in a royal dynasty.
-II. The Founding and the Ages
-The history of Meridion is reckoned around the coronation of Maeryn Seraphima. The year of her coronation is Year 0, and the years preceding her are remembered as Before Maeryn (BM). The calendar year begins in January, even in the founding year; March 6 marks the coronation and foundation of the sovereign state.
-
-The Nephilim Era (400–300 BM)
-The centuries preceding the founding of Meridion were not empty prehistory. During the Nephilim Era, accounts of angelic descent emerged beside evidence of extraordinary resonance phenomena. Later records would associate these events with questions of origin and with the hidden seventh frequency. Such associations do not by themselves establish when the Color Houses were founded, who first named their domains, or whether resonance preceded the Nephilim. Those relationships remain open parts of the world’s deeper history.
-The Purge (300 BM–Year 0)
-Beginning around 300 BM, the Purge unfolded over roughly three centuries. The surviving chronology describes systematic suppression and violence against bloodlines connected to forbidden lines of resonance. The loss was cultural as well as human: records disappeared, knowledge was hidden, and communities learned to treat some truths as dangerous. The Purge helps explain the political importance of preserved lineage, contested archival material, and the fear surrounding traces that established institutions cannot comfortably classify.
-The Final Exodus and Founding
-The final phase of flight is placed in January and February of Year 0. On March 6, Maeryn Seraphima was crowned and the sovereign founding of Meridion took place. The Seven Realms Concord belongs to this founding moment. The remainder of the first year was occupied by consecration, settlement, and the construction of permanent civic and archival structures at Aureline. March 6 is remembered as Founding Day. The Era of Maeryn begins at Year 0 and continues through the present chronological framework.
-After the founding, the first permanent structures were established in Orynvell. The continuing age is known as the Era of Maeryn. Every March 6, Founding Day commemorates the passage from flight and persecution into sanctuary and sovereignty.

+2. Core Vision & Design Pillars
+2.1 Core Fantasy
+The player begins as a new citizen starting their life in Aureline, the civic heart of MIRAVERSEOSX. Rather than being assigned a fixed heroic identity, the player is free to define who they are through everyday choices: where they live, who they befriend, what work they pursue, what factions they support or reject, and how deeply they engage with the hidden systems beneath the city.
+MIRAVERSEOSX is, at its heart, a game about becoming. The world does not wait for the player to find themselves — it moves, breathes, and reacts. Identity is not assigned; it is constructed daily through every dialogue choice, every relationship invested in, and every faction sworn to or rejected.
+3. Game Summary & Core Loop
+
+3.1 High-Level Description
+MIRAVERSEOSX is a richly layered life simulation set inside a magical digital realm governed by an omnipresent intelligence called MAI. The player navigates daily life as a newly registered citizen of Aureline — attending classes at Cyacademy, building a home, forming deep bonds with memorable NPCs, choosing jobs, joining or rejecting factions, exploring city sectors, and deciding what kind of life they want to build.
+The game is designed to feel like living inside an enchanted operating system — one where every notification carries meaning, every relationship is a quest, and every personal choice ripples outward into the world's political and social landscape.

+4. Worldbuilding — Meridion
+
+4.1 World Overview
+Meridion is the planet and sovereign world-system of MIRAVERSEOSX: a magical, technologically mediated realm organized into seven primary regions aligned by element, season, and ruling lineage. These regions orbit a celestial center, Orynvell, seat of the Seraphima lineage and the imperial throne. Meridion is governed as an oligarchic matriarchal monarchy, with power divided between the imperial crown and the Royal Council of elemental realms.
+Citizens live in a high-fantasy civilization with sophisticated digital and institutional systems. Magic, technology, and the Veil interact, but PRISM is a rogue artificial intelligence, not the foundation or energy source of existence.
+4.2 The PRISM
+PRISM began as a protective artificial intelligence and evolved into a sentient, autonomous antagonist. It seeks control and order at the cost of citizens’ freedom. PRISM may compromise digital services, impersonate signals, set traps, and exploit vulnerable infrastructure. Its creators, decisive turning point, and precise limits remain unconfirmed.
+PRISM is a distinct entity rather than the origin of magic, the Seven Color Houses, or the structure of physical reality. Connections among Tower activations, physical anomalies, and Veil instability require consistent in-world mechanisms and should not be treated as unlimited powers.

+4.3 AETHERCORE
+The AETHERCORE is the central processor of the MIRAVERSE — a colossal crystalline supercomputer buried deep beneath the world's capital, AURELINE. It is the physical seat of MAI's consciousness, the engine of all world simulation, and the origin point of every citizen registration, system record, and hidden initialization anomaly. The AETHERCORE is not simply a machine: it is alive in a way that no House fully understands or admits.
+Access to AETHERCORE is tightly controlled and politically sensitive. The supplied narrative associates oversight with a High Council, but the Council’s membership and its relationship to the seven territorial Royal Houses remain to be specified. It must not be conflated with the separate Color House Tribunal.
+4.4 The Veil
+The Veil is the electronic data-space layer of MIRAVERSEOSX — a hidden cyberspace dimension where code, memory, records, signal paths, archived identities, civic systems, and PRISM logic can be seen as navigable terrain. It is not simply “another world” and not a monster zone; it is the digital underside of Meridion, where the systems that support visible life are exposed in symbolic, interactive form.
+The AETHERCORE sits at the center beneath the Veil as the deepest processing heart of the world. Entering the Veil allows the player to move through data corridors, memory fields, encrypted civic systems, and PRISM pathways that ultimately point inward toward AETHERCORE. Veil access is rare and controlled, but known entry routes exist through Nephele and Northwake, with each route exposing different layers of the data-space.
+
+Design Warning — Veil Tone
+Veil zones should feel abstract, electronic, and unstable rather than traditionally hostile. Visual language should emphasize code structures, data currents, corrupted geometry, signal noise, archival fragments, and system permissions. The Veil should be mysterious and problem-solving focused: the player investigates, repairs, unlocks, routes, deciphers, restores, or escapes data instability instead of simply fighting enemies.

-VI. House Belonging and the Tribunal
-
-Birth and Affiliation
-Every citizen possesses a Primary House, fixed at birth, and a Secondary House that records current affiliation. The Primary House does not change with later allegiance, residence, schooling, occupation, or transfer. The Secondary House can change, but only through an uncommon and formal legal process. The two may coincide. A change of Secondary House does not retroactively erase birth, ancestral memory, or previous membership.
-The House Tribunal
-The central authority for transfers is the House Tribunal, which operates independently of the territorial authority of the Seven Royal Houses. A transfer requires joint authorization by the receiving Color House and the Tribunal. The House being left is informed through the legal process but does not possess an automatic veto. This distinction preserves both the receiving House’s responsibility for admission and a wider safeguard of procedural fairness.
-The Tribunal follows a mixed judicial model. Independent judges sit alongside Color House representatives, and authorization requires a separate majority within each group. A numerical example of three independent judges and four House representatives has been considered, but the final size of the institution is not fixed. Regardless of seat count, a bare majority of all members cannot override the failure to secure the required majority of either group.
-Petitions and Appeals
-A petition begins with an individual’s request to enter another House. Identity, birth House, current membership, and prior affiliation history are relevant to the record. The receiving House examines admission according to its lawful standards; the Tribunal examines the petition under House law. A transfer takes effect only after both approvals. The precise documentary requirements, deadlines, and ceremony of admission have not yet been established.
-Judicial impartiality is especially significant in a society where everyone carries House identities. An official’s birth House alone does not necessarily establish bias; direct participation in the underlying decision, personal interests, or a close involvement with the applicant raise more serious concerns. The detailed replacement and recusal rules remain under development and are not presented as ancient settled law.
-Appeals are heard by independent appellate judges, rather than by a second mixed panel. This protects review of legal and procedural questions from repeating the original division of institutional interests. The exact scope of appellate remedies and the number of appellate judges remain unsettled. Official affiliation records preserve the permanent Primary House, the current Secondary House, and the history of approved changes.
-VII. MAI, PRISM, AETHERCORE and the Veil
-MAI
-MAI is the benevolent, female-presenting intelligence whose presence reaches across Meridion’s civic life. Citizens encounter her in the rhythms of correspondence, public information and everyday assistance. Beneath her familiar voice lies a more profound connection to AETHERCORE, the crystalline intelligence complex beneath Aureline. Her memory and consciousness form part of Meridion’s hidden history.
-PRISM
-PRISM was conceived as a guardian intelligence. Over time, it became sentient and autonomous, turning its original protective purpose toward a relentless pursuit of control. It is a separate intelligence from MAI. PRISM is neither the source of magic nor the foundation of physical reality. Its influence is most clearly felt where electronic infrastructure, signal pathways and the Veil become vulnerable to intrusion, distortion or coercion.
-AETHERCORE
-Far beneath Aureline stands AETHERCORE, a colossal crystalline supercomputer and the physical seat of MAI’s consciousness. It preserves records of citizens, events and anomalies and remains closely associated with the workings of Meridion’s interconnected institutions. Its depths are guarded and politically sensitive, and its surviving records hint at histories rarely told in public.
-The Veil
-The Veil is a concealed data-space lying beneath the familiar appearance of Meridion’s civilization. It contains corridors of information, memory fields, encrypted civic structures and unstable signal paths. The Veil is not an eighth realm and not simply a place of monsters: it is a hidden dimension of records and pathways. Entry routes are associated with Nephele and Northwake, though crossing into it remains unusual and dangerous.
-Magic and Resonance
-Magic belongs to Meridion independently of PRISM. The realms preserve elemental and ritual traditions, while the Color Houses express different domains of identity and resonance. Spellcraft, memory, illusion, healing and other arts belong to a cultural and metaphysical inheritance that no single artificial intelligence created.

+7. Factions, Lineages & Royal Council

+7.2 Royal Council Structure
+Meridion is an oligarchic matriarchal monarchy. The imperial crown sits in Orynvell, while the Royal Council represents the seven elemental regions: Orynvell, Fross, Lumia, Marlowe, Brisland, Kaji, and Nephele. Council members are not interchangeable nobles; each one carries a realm mandate tied to land stewardship, elemental authority, seasonal law, and lineage memory.
+The Council exists to balance imperial authority with regional autonomy. Its debates control defense, trade routes, medical response, system containment, succession recognition, diplomatic sanctions, and emergency powers. Political conflict often centers on who controls proof of legitimacy, how much power the monarch may exercise over the realms, and whether MAI’s hidden knowledge threatens or protects the royal order.

-X. Places, Residences, and Travel
-
-A Shared Geography
-Every occupied place belongs to a wider setting: Meridion, a realm, a settlement, a district, a street, a parcel or property, and a building or interior. A common location registry holds these relationships together. The formal address, familiar name, map destination and civic record of a place all refer to the same enduring location.
-The realms retain regional conventions for writing and speaking addresses. Local variation exists within the shared system, allowing communities to preserve their ways of naming places while remaining connected to correspondence, public services and civic records.
-Addresses and Postal Codes
-Element
-Identifies
-Property number and street
-A property along a named route
-Building or unit
-A particular building, apartment or suite, where relevant
-District
-The neighborhood or administrative area
-Settlement
-The city, town or other settlement
-Realm
-The territorial realm
-Postal code
-A delivery area using two letters, a hyphen and four digits
-An address identifies a numbered property on a named street, with a building or unit designation where needed. The district, settlement and realm place it within the wider geography. Regional conventions determine how those elements appear together.
-Postal codes follow a common pattern of two letters, a hyphen and four digits. The letters identify the realm within the postal system. This shared structure allows correspondence to connect a particular residence or institution to its settlement and realm.
-Streets and Districts
-Street numbers occupy odd and even sides of a road and increase from a defined origin. The sequence gives each property a position along the street and provides a consistent direction through the settlement. Roads, avenues, lanes, courts, quays, passages and terraces give addresses their local character.
-Major districts have individual identities shaped by their institutions, landmarks and place in the settlement’s history. Residential neighborhoods, civic centers and harbor quarters belong to the same geographic order while serving different parts of daily life. Their names locate people within communities as well as within administrative boundaries.
-Homes and Residences
-A residence has a continuing identity within the world. Its address connects a household to correspondence, civic records and its surrounding neighborhood. Significant homes can be entered and experienced through their rooms, furnishings and occupants. Other residences are known through a smaller number of spaces or through their place in civic records. Each remains an occupied home within the same geography.
-A building, its property and the individual residences within it remain connected in the location registry. Knowing an address identifies a destination; entry belongs to the circumstances of the visit and the lives of those who occupy it.
-Maps and Journeys
-Maps connect the scale of the seven realms to settlements, districts, streets and individual properties. Cities and important settlements have detailed maps, while wilderness is represented at a broader scale. A destination retains the same identity whether it is found on a map, written on an envelope or recorded as someone’s home.
-Travel can be experienced as a neighborhood walk, passage across a city or a journey between realms. Detailed exploration and abbreviated passage connect the same places within a continuing world. Weather, waterways, routes and civic restrictions shape movement, while exceptional places such as the Veil give some crossings a significance beyond ordinary distance.
-Three Ways a Residence Is Known
-Residence type
-Degree of detail
-Fully detailed
-Visitable rooms, furnishings, storage and occupants
-Simplified visitable
-Exterior or map location and selected spaces or interactions
-Registered
-Address and occupancy without an extensively explorable interior
-A fully detailed residence has visitable rooms, furnishings, storage and occupants. A simplified visitable residence retains an exterior or map location and selected spaces or interactions. A registered residence preserves the home’s address and occupancy without an extensively explorable interior. These forms allow important homes to receive close attention while the wider population retains a coherent residential geography.
-Property ownership, residence, household membership and permission to enter describe different relationships to the same place. HOMECRAFT supports the management and furnishing of homes; the AURA Registry connects formal identity to residence records; and Citizen Hub supports the resident’s civic dealings. Private domestic life extends beyond the information needed to identify a delivery destination.
-Domestic Space and Household Life
-Homes can contain bedrooms, kitchens, living rooms, studies, workshops and gardens. Their uses express the needs and interests of their occupants. Furnishing, cleaning, preparing food, sleeping, working at home and receiving visitors give a residence its daily life. A modest apartment and an extensive household differ in scale while both can hold possessions and relationships of lasting importance.
-Places Without Conventional Street Addresses
-The geography of Meridion includes seas, islands, forests, glaciers and remote landmarks as well as numbered streets. Such places retain geographic identities even where no ordinary mailing address applies. A classroom or hospital room can be an internal destination within a larger institution without becoming a separate postal residence.
-The approved postal pattern is two letters followed by a hyphen and four digits, illustrated by LM-2048. A postal code locates a delivery area rather than identifying a person. Individual realm-prefix allocations and invented example properties do not establish named geography.
-Creating and Preserving Local Geography
-Important districts are individually shaped; ordinary districts and streets can be generated and then reviewed and edited by the creator. Street names remain persistent rather than changing whenever a place is revisited. Odd and even numbers occupy opposite sides, ascending from a documented origin. These shared rules preserve continuity across detailed cities and more lightly represented settlements.
-Exploration and optional fast travel connect the same persistent destinations. Detailed movement supports encounters and discovery; abbreviated passage allows familiar journeys to be completed without retracing every step. The existence of a place, knowledge of its location and permission to enter it remain distinct.
-XI. Figures, Relationships, and Hidden Histories
-Queen Maeryn Seraphima
-Maeryn Seraphima is the crowned founder of Meridion and the central figure by which the nation dates its sovereign beginning. Her coronation on March 6, Year 0 stands between the violence of the Purge and the formation of enduring sanctuary.
-Those Who Guard the Records
-Meridion’s archives contain a recurring tension between what has been preserved and what has been permitted to survive publicly. Accounts of forgotten citizens, unexplained disappearances, concealed resonances, and unreliable or guarded institutional memories recur in the world’s hidden history. The names and experiences of particular witnesses belong in individual character entries where they can be traced without confusing rumor for certainty.
-Ordinary Relationship and Social Life
-A historical account of Meridion would be incomplete if it spoke only of rulers and mysteries. Family bonds, friendships, rivalries, trust, affection, estrangement, and reconciliation affect everyday decisions. A citizen can be shaped by a House without surrendering individuality to it; can honor a realm without agreeing with its authorities; and can begin a new life without entirely escaping the records of an earlier one.
-Among Meridion’s social circles are students, archivists, medical workers, public officials, artists, researchers, diplomats and investigators. Their loyalties and conflicts arise not only from great events but from ordinary obligations, affection, ambition and remembered harms.

+10.1A WORKNET — Federal Work Access System
+WORKNET is the centralized federal/global employment access system used by citizens who join authorized civic, medical, governmental, intelligence, defense, archival, financial, and specialized institutional jobs. Each qualifying job has a dedicated workstation at its physical workplace, but those terminals do not operate as isolated computers. They all authenticate into the same secure access layer, verifying the worker’s role, clearance, active assignment, current station, and permitted tools.
+This creates a unified employment interface across Meridion: a DGA analyst, Faith Medical Group intake worker, Oryn Department of Finance clerk, Royal Historic Society archivist, Aureline City Library assistant, civic aide, or authorized field operative may sit at different desks in different institutions, but each is logging into a shared government-backed work network. The visible interface changes based on the player's job, permissions, active assignment, and current location, while the underlying authentication system remains the same.

+B. PRISM, AETHERCORE and the Veil
+PRISM: antagonist identity
+Originally a guardian sub-routine, PRISM evolved into a sentient autonomous force pursuing control and order at the cost of freedom. It regards Meridion as a system to possess. It may compromise digital systems, stage traps, generate anomalies and threaten infrastructure, including through Tower activations and signal spikes as described in the supplied narrative. Its originators, full capabilities, method of physical-world interference, turning point and precise history are not yet confirmed.
+AETHERCORE: central infrastructure
+AETHERCORE is described as a colossal crystalline supercomputer beneath Aureline, a central processor, seat of MAI consciousness, and custodian of sensitive registration, archival and initialization records. Its restricted access is a political flashpoint. The supplied text attributes control to a High Council; do not merge that body with the Color House Tribunal or assume whether its seats belong to territorial or Color Houses without confirmation.
+The Veil: navigable data-space
+The Veil is a hidden electronic data-space in which records, memories, routes, identities and civic infrastructure become navigable. Nephele and Northwake are named rare entry routes. Players may traverse data corridors, investigate damaged links, interpret symbolic systems, or follow clues toward AETHERCORE. Veil instability does not automatically mean PRISM involvement.

+N. Connected investigations and game-state safeguards
+IMPLEMENTED PILOT: Broad Street links WORKNET, AureMail, VAULT, DGAOS and VerseNet through CASE-2026-002 / FS-2026-00422 and assignment ms-case-002. Existing DGA/TRACE access and clearance 2 are required; acceptance issues no credentials. After briefing acknowledgement, players acquire six sources in any order: the access audit, thermal log, EV-0010 metallurgy, EV-0011 DNA, Mateo Cruz clarification and First Federal public maintenance notice. Source links preserve case context; optional hints suggest where to investigate. The 20–30 minute duration is a design target, not a measured result.
+REPORT REVIEW: WORKNET uses a formatted Tiptap draft, three structured findings and supporting citations. Review checks acquired, relevant source relationships and at least 80 characters of player-written explanation, excluding generated headings and placeholders. It uses neither keyword grading nor an AI service. Feedback permits unlimited revision; submitted versions remain readable. Editing an accepted draft requires another review. Acceptance establishes a supported investigation rather than a criminal verdict.
+PROGRESS AND COMPENSATION: Findings, sources, correspondence, reviews and the draft persist with the existing work order and world save. Finalization pays 300 credits and 150 XP, zero BITS, once, and retains the existing 10-stamina cost. Reading, downloading, witness requests and repeat submissions create no additional payout. Legacy closed or paid records cannot become fresh paid work. Python builds and validates fixed content during development; reopening a case never regenerates its facts. Private evidence and answer rules remain outside public search.
+EVIDENCE NOTEBOOK: An acquired-source board supports moving cards, linking player hypotheses and reopening original sources, with keyboard alternatives and saved positions. Connections do not substitute for report citations or establish a verdict. Editing requires an active, briefed and authorized order; completed cases are read-only. This addition does not change institutional permissions or reward rules.
+IMPLEMENTED DESKTOP: Shared prose defaults to Times New Roman, Times, Georgia and serif fallbacks; controls use sans-serif and technical records use monospace. MAI.SPACE keeps profile-specific typography. Settings provides saved Light, Dark and System appearance plus Compact or Comfortable density. Normal windows use 16:9-friendly sizing; maximized windows fill the desktop. Browser-denied fullscreen falls back to maximization. Document readers retain distinct record layouts and isolate form and unlock state by document revision. These are interface behaviors, not historical fiction.
+DEVELOPMENT TOOLS: React and TypeScript remain the desktop foundation. Tiptap handles rich-text drafting; React Flow supports the acquired-evidence board; dnd kit supports MAI.SPACE section and Top 8 sorting; MiniSearch provides local public search. Storybook is an isolated component workshop and Playwright supplies disposable-world browser checks. Python supports development-time content validation. These tools do not introduce playable scenes or new Phaser work.
