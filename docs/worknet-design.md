# WORKNET: Complete Design & Decision Report

MiraverseOSx · October 23, 2026 · Design status: Planning · GitHub unchanged

This report consolidates everything we've decided about WORKNET's visual redesign, institutional structure, employment simulation, workplace activities, NPC colleagues, professional progression, and career management.

It also distinguishes approved decisions from proposed features that still need further development.

## 1. Executive summary

WORKNET will become Meridion's centralized government, corporate, and professional employment network.

It will no longer function primarily as a job board or mission launcher. Instead, it will provide a complete employment environment where citizens can search for jobs, apply for positions, manage multiple employers, perform interactive professional duties, build careers, develop workplace relationships, and progress through institutional hierarchies.

## 3. Visual design and appearance

We explicitly rejected the nostalgic 2000s aesthetic for WORKNET. That aesthetic belongs to MAI.SPACE.

WORKNET's intended appearance is closer to a professional government personnel portal or corporate operations platform.

The working palette consists of warm ivory, muted sage, forest green, charcoal, and restrained heather/plum accents. Exact color values remain proposed rather than locked.

The layout should feature clear typography, thin borders, professional navigation, structured tables, restrained cards, consistent status indicators, and balanced spacing.

The current two-panel opportunity browser is worth preserving and refining: a searchable list on the left and detailed assignment information on the right. A separate overview dashboard can provide summaries.

### Workplace Switcher

Option A is confirmed. A central organization selector changes the active workplace without launching a completely separate application.

A citizen could switch between DGA, Faith Medical, and Cyacademy, provided they have valid affiliations.

Switching workplaces changes the displayed institutional environment and available functions. It does not terminate other employment, erase progress, or automatically change the citizen's active physical location.

## 6. Multi-employer careers

WORKNET will recognize that a citizen may hold more than one job.

Each position can have its own employer, title, department, contract, schedule, supervisor, workplace permissions, assignments, and performance history.

A central career profile ties these professional experiences together.

The intended rules are:

- Employment records remain distinct between employers.
- Scheduling conflicts and professional incompatibilities matter.
- An employee's authorization does not transfer automatically between institutions.
- Qualifications and verifiable experience can remain part of the citizen's career history after leaving a job.
- Institutional records retain their own privacy and access restrictions.

The detailed rules for simultaneous full-time employment, conflicts of interest, and labor arrangements have not yet been finalized.

## 7. Hiring and employment lifecycle

WORKNET is designed to support a complete professional lifecycle.

1. Career discovery: Browse occupations and opportunities
2. Recruitment: Apply, submit qualifications, interview, or complete assessments
3. Onboarding: Receive an approved position, institutional affiliation, and necessary access
4. Employment: Perform daily duties and professional assignments
5. Development: Gain experience, complete training, build relationships, receive evaluations
6. Career transition: Seek promotion, transfer, change employers, take leave, resign, or retire

Permanent employment, short-term contracts, internships, civic assignments, and specialist missions will remain distinct opportunities with different procedures.

## 8. Full Workplace Life simulation

Option C is confirmed.

This is one of our most significant decisions. A workplace will have ongoing routines, not just a sequence of available jobs.

Full Workplace Life includes daily schedules, professional duties, colleagues, supervisors, meetings, workplace locations, evaluations, professional development, personal obligations, and employment consequences.

Players should be able to experience an ordinary workday as well as major professional storylines.

Workplaces should support office activity, departmental workstations, meetings, break rooms, records departments, and field locations where appropriate.

Not every profession needs the same physical environment or working hours.

## 9. Hybrid authored and dynamic work

Option C is confirmed.

WORKNET will combine three sources of professional activity.

| Layer         | Description                                                   | Example                                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Routine work  | Duties associated with a position and schedule                | Reviewing records and preparing daily reports                      |
| Dynamic work  | Responsibilities arising from changing workplace conditions   | Covering a colleague's absence or addressing a new service request |
| Authored work | Carefully constructed professional stories and major missions | The connected Broad Street Investigation                           |

Dynamic work may respond to staffing, workload, world events, deadlines, equipment condition, resources, qualifications, and supervisory decisions.

Important authored missions remain coherent and should not be randomly overwritten or prematurely resolved by routine simulation.

Generated assignments must also avoid duplicate rewards or endless low-value work.

| Medicine       | Role-authorized patient assessment, chart review, clinical documentation |
| Education      | Lesson preparation, academic evaluation, student support                 |
| Research       | Study planning, analysis, documentation, scholarly reporting             |

## 11. NPC coworkers and workplace relationships

NPC colleagues should be independent participants in the professional world.

They will have their own responsibilities, personalities, professional ambitions, schedules, relationships, and experiences.

We developed five proposed dimensions of professional relationships: trust, competence recognition, familiarity, cooperation, and conflict history.

NPCs may collaborate with the player, compete for opportunities, offer assistance, request help, disagree over professional judgments, or develop mentor relationships.

Their work may continue when the player is absent, but major player-centered authored events should not be resolved offscreen accidentally.

The precise NPC simulation rules and relationship calculations remain to be defined.

## 12. Supervisors and organizational authority

Supervisors are intended to be active decision-makers.

Their responsibilities include distributing work, reviewing reports, approving appropriate requests, conducting evaluations, mentoring staff, managing schedules, and responding to departmental needs.

We developed examples of supervisors handling staffing shortages by redistributing duties, adjusting priorities, seeking extensions, or requesting additional assistance.

Supervisory decisions must respect actual institutional authority. They should not provide unlimited clearance, instant qualifications, or unearned credits.

Leadership gameplay is still open. We discussed supervisory, departmental, executive, and business-owner roles, but you have not formally selected the leadership model.

## 13. Employment schedules and workplace routines

WORKNET should accommodate multiple employment arrangements.

These include full-time, part-time, flexible, shift-based, contract, on-call, and potentially self-employed work.

Schedules may contain mandatory responsibilities, meetings, project deadlines, breaks, and approved leave.

A workplace can experience ordinary interruptions or unexpected changes without becoming an emergency-driven system.

Our proposed scheduling rules include checking conflicts between simultaneous jobs, respecting approved leave, and applying consequences proportionately when obligations are missed.

## 14. Career progression and qualifications

Progression remains grounded in the larger MiraverseOSx gameplay system.

Career XP alone cannot substitute for education, licensing, verified skills, professional qualifications, reputation, required experience, and institutional approval.

Promotion might require a suitable vacancy, satisfactory evaluations, professional development, supervisory recommendations, and relevant credentials.

We also discussed two broad career pathways: specialist advancement and leadership advancement.

That distinction matters because an excellent investigator, physician, researcher, or engineer should be able to become more accomplished without necessarily becoming a manager.

Exact professional titles and promotion ladders are not yet canon.

## 15. Personnel records

WORKNET should maintain both a central professional history and employer-specific personnel files.

| Central career record      | Institutional personnel file       |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Employment history         | Specific employment contract       |
| Verified qualifications    | Department assignment              |
| Professional experience    | Supervisor and reporting structure |
| Completed training         | Internal evaluations               |
| Recognized accomplishments | Role-specific access               |
| Career transitions         | Authorized internal records        |

Professional history should survive resignation and career changes, while confidential employer records remain appropriately restricted.

Employment affiliations may connect to issued institutional AureMail accounts and authorized Comms channels.

Leaving an organization should end permissions that depend on active employment without deleting legitimate historical accomplishments.

## 16. Performance, consequences, and recovery

WORKNET preserves the previously approved graduated consequences model.

A minor mistake might require correction or coaching. Repeated poor performance could lead to a performance improvement process or reduced responsibility. Serious misconduct could result in formal disciplinary procedures.

Good work can improve trust, evaluations, and eligibility for opportunities, but does not automatically guarantee promotion.

Appropriate recovery mechanisms should exist, including revised submissions, retraining, improved performance, appeals where applicable, and restored standing.

Not every missed deadline should become a disciplinary crisis, and approved leave should not count as poor performance.

## 19. Design decisions versus implementation

The discussion has established WORKNET's direction, not completed its implementation.

The existing WORKNET application and its connected assignments provide a foundation. However, the complete workplace simulation, personnel systems, employer switching behavior, autonomous coworkers, schedules, and detailed career lifecycle described here should be treated as design specifications until individually implemented and tested.

No changes have been made to GitHub as part of this discussion.

## Final consolidated vision

WORKNET is a unified employment and professional-life system for Meridion. Citizens discover opportunities, join institutions, build qualifications, fulfill daily responsibilities, collaborate with coworkers, receive evaluations, maintain professional records, and develop careers over time.

Its appearance is modern, restrained, and institutional. Government agencies and private organizations share a consistent interface while retaining distinct branding, access permissions, and workplace environments.

Its gameplay combines authored professional stories with dynamic department activity, making ordinary employment as much a part of the living world as major investigations and adventures.

Design status: WORKNET is paused here, with the current decisions consolidated. Future development can resume from this report without reopening the choices we've already made.
