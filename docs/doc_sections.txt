6.4 Starter Progression Loop
========================================
The starter progression loop covers the first stretch of a new save, when the player has nothing and must activate each basic system manually. Its purpose is to teach the operating system, introduce the blank-slate premise, and turn onboarding into gameplay instead of a static tutorial.
