## Scope and independence

Journey > Adventure > Mission > Quest > Task describes scope, not mandatory nesting. Missions can directly contain Tasks; Quests can stand alone. Shared evidence may advance several systems, but the same accomplishment receives its base reward once.

Tasks are assigned individual objectives requiring actual player action. Missions are structured assignments with explicit objectives and evaluation. Quests are goal-oriented and can permit different approaches. Adventures connect events, branches and persistent consequences. Journeys are broad life paths; chapters and milestones can finish while the Journey remains open.

Cooking, sleeping, shopping, cleaning, decorating, socializing and exploring can happen without becoming formal objectives. Familiarity can simplify manually chosen routines. Ordinary rest is conditional, advances time, and does not farm XP; special dreams may introduce lore or discoveries.

## Discovery presentation

- Identified opportunity: immediate optional Quest notification.
- Unexplained object: Discovery Journal entry.
- Mysterious event: contextual clue without a Quest announcement.
- Developed investigation: Adventure reveal or invitation.
- Hidden location: exploration reward without requiring a Quest.

Secrets can depend on knowledge, relationships, exploration or previous accomplishments. Discoveries never have to generate objectives. Adventures may develop through authored scenes or emergent combinations of events, employment, exploration and social connections.

## Competence, consequences and integrity

Promotion cannot depend on XP alone. Relevant skills, verified experience and appropriate education, certification, reputation or institutional approval matter. Individual professions require meaningful mechanics. Graduated consequences may affect quality, resources, timing, trust or opportunities; corrective work and recovery can be possible.

Retain WORKNET-002, HOMECRAFT-001, FAITH-003 and MAI.SPACE-004. HOMECRAFT purchases deduct established costs and grant +40 XP and +10 comfort once. Personal healthcare, one-time social onboarding, ongoing relationships and work rewards remain separately scoped.
